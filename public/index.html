What your child can explore

Practical programs designed for students to explore, build, experiment and solve real-world problems. Here is a glimpse of what they learn.

  • Artificial Intelligence

    • Python for AI and data handling
    • Computer vision and image processing
    • Machine learning: training and testing models
    • AI-powered robots and drones

  • Robotics

    • Motors, sensors and mechanisms
    • Line-following and obstacle-avoiding robots
    • Wireless and app-controlled robots
    • Robotic arms and autonomous navigation

  • Coding & Programming

    • Block coding to Arduino programming
    • Logic: conditions, loops and functions
    • Building mobile apps to control robots
    • Python for data and AI

  • STEM & Innovation

    • Electronics and circuit fundamentals
    • Safe, responsible lab practice
    • Smart IoT: sensors, cloud and dashboards
    • Troubleshooting and improving designs

  • Building Real Projects

    • Build working robots from scratch
    • Smart automation and IoT prototypes
    • Team robotics challenges
    • A final capstone project to showcase

Frequently asked questions

Which grades are the programs for?

EdLumina's AI & Robotics programs are designed for students in Grades 3–12.

What program formats are available?

We offer after-school and weekend programs. In the form you can choose either one, or let us suggest the best fit.

What are the batch timings?

Weekday batches run from 4 PM to 6 PM and 6 PM to 8 PM, and there are Sunday options too. Tell us your preference in the form and we'll confirm availability.

Is the demo or counselling session free?

Yes. You can book a free demo or counselling session through the form, or ask for more information first.

How will I get fee details?

After you register, our team will contact you with program details, batch timings and fee information suitable for your child.
